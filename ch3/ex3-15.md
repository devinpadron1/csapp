# Exercise 3.15

In the following excerpts from a disassembled binary, some information has been replaced by `X`s. Answer the questions below.

1. What is the target of the `je` instruction? You do not need to analyze the `callq` instruction.

   ```asm
   4003fa: 74 02       je XXXXXX
   4003fc: ff d0       callq *%rax
   ```

2. What is the target of this `je` instruction?

   ```asm
   40042f: 74 f4       je XXXXXX
   400431: 5d          pop %rbp
   ```

3. What are the addresses of the `ja` and `pop` instructions?

   ```asm
   XXXXXX: 77 02       ja 400547
   XXXXXX: 5d          pop %rbp
   ```

4. The jump target below is encoded in PC-relative form as a 4-byte two's-complement number. The bytes are listed from least significant to most significant. What is the address of the jump target?

   ```asm
   4005e8: e9 73 ff ff ff    jmpq XXXXXXX
   4005ed: 90                nop
   ```
